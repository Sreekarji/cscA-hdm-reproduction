================================================================================
NOTES ON tpc=10
================================================================================

At tpc=10 (50 concurrent tasks), PPO outperforms HDM (0.523 vs 0.428). Under
extreme congestion the allocation problem simplifies — bandwidth should be
spread uniformly since no single task can be prioritised without starving
others. PPO's on-policy Gaussian updates converge to this uniform strategy
faster within the 1000-episode budget. HDM's 6-step reverse diffusion over a
50-task action space requires more exploration to reach the same conclusion.
This is consistent with the paper's finding that HDM's advantage is largest in
the medium-congestion regime where intent competition is meaningful.
